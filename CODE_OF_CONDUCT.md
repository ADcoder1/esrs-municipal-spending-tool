# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes careful, respectful collaboration across public-sector,
research, software, and sustainability communities.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Avoid sharing confidential or identifying data.
- Welcome domain expertise from finance, procurement, sustainability, IT, and
  municipal operations.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, disclosure of
private data, or knowingly misleading claims about sustainability performance.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the
community and the people represented in the data.
