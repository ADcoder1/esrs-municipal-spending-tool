Use this tool to: sort municipal purchases into environmental topics, find categories that need more information, and prepare examples to discuss with colleagues. It does not calculate environmental impact or make formal ESRS disclosures. Excel support reads the first worksheet in the uploaded file.

How the tool made the suggested links

Numbers below are purchase rows, not amounts. Confidence describes the strength of the category link, not environmental performance.

  • High: a category-guide entry marked as high confidence matched the purchase.
  • Medium: a category-guide entry marked as medium confidence matched the purchase. Use it as a starting point where context remains broad.
  • Needs review: no suitable category-guide entry was found, a keyword suggested the link, or the guide itself flags it for review.

Working-level purchasing topics under E1-E5

E1-E5 are the five broad ESRS environmental themes. The labels below are practical procurement topics used by this tool to make those themes useful for municipal spending. They are not official ESRS disclosure subcategories. Numbers show purchase rows.
