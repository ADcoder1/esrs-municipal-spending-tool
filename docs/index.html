ESRS Municipal Spending Tool

Browser-only demo for classifying municipal procurement and invoice data against ESRS environmental themes. This public page uses synthetic sample data. If you upload local CSV files, they are processed in your browser and are not sent to a server.

Prototype scope: This demo is for exploring classification logic and review workflows. It does not calculate environmental performance or produce formal ESRS disclosures.
